## 9 · Zoom · Discovery

# What happens before it reaches THE MAP?

*The discovery half, before anything reaches the map.*

| | Does | Produces |
|---|---|---|
| **Public sources** | Monafasat · Tarmeez · Saudi Made · Commerce · HRSD · Contractors Authority | A name and a CR number |
| **The Detective** · searches, and reaches out | Reads the site, the register and the awards. And contacts whoever it finds | A supplier with its evidence |
| **The Auditor** · checks | Audits what was found by the Detective. Classifies and documents the supplier | A documented capability, onto THE MAP |

### Outreach by the Detective

The Detective is also the outreach: it finds the supplier and it is what reaches them. Every message carries the **MUSAHAMA registration link**, and each stage runs only on whoever did not answer the last, so the count falls at every step and the expensive channel stays cheap in total.

| Stage | Channel | | Cost per contact | Who is in it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Email | Carries the registration link · we wait 5 days | `~ $0.0001` | Everyone we found |
| 2 | WhatsApp | Same link · we wait 3 days | `~ $0.04` | Who did not answer the email |
| 3 | Voice call | An AI voice agent calls | `< $1.00` | Who did not answer WhatsApp |

The link goes to MUSAHAMA's own registration. KAMIN keeps no login, no upload and no approval queue.

## 10 · Zoom · The Decision

# And when THE MAP says nobody can?

*And what happens where the map says nobody can.*

`THE MAP → The Advisor → Gap register → Coverage rate`

**The Advisor** does not stop at "nobody can". It walks six rungs in order, and only drops a rung once the one above it fails.

| | Decision | Instrument |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Buy it** | Purchase order |
| 2 | **Split it** | Split award |
| 3 | **Invest** | Capacity against offtake |
| 4 | **Partner** | Partnership and transfer |
| 5 | **Localise** | Direct investment |
| 6 | **Import** | Import |

Rungs 3, 4 and 5 are PIF's own instruments, and 3 and 4 are **asked of the supplier, not inferred**: "could you serve this if we expanded your capacity?", "could you bring a global manufacturer in with you?". The question travels the outreach cascade, and **an unanswered rung is not a no**.

**Gap register:** what stayed unsolved, booked at its annual value.
**Coverage rate:** what we could serve locally, after every rung.

## 11 · The map

# KAMIN's map of local industrial capability

| Limb | Chain |
|---|---|
| Discovery | `Public sources → The Detective → The Auditor · capability audit → THE MAP` |
| Demand | `Purchase requests → The Coordinator → The Auditor · request audit → THE MAP` |
| Decision | `THE MAP → The Advisor → Gap register → Coverage rate` |

The Auditor sits beside the map and works in two passes, not one. The capability audit writes to the map; the request audit only reads it.

**THE MAP:** baseline + capability + pooled demand. The baseline is everyone PIF already knows, and it is still the minority. Everything above that line is **discovery lift**, computed rather than asserted.
