The Advisor
They do not stop at the gap. They hand you a ladder of options, cheapest first.
1
Buy it
One supplier can serve the volume today
2
Split it
Several local suppliers together, so the volume does not exclude the small ones
3
Invest
“Could you, if we expanded your capacity?” Capex, against a contract for the volume
4
Partner
“Could you bring a global manufacturer in with you?” A joint venture, and knowledge transfer
5
Localise
Nobody can, so PIF brings the industry itself into the Kingdom
6
Import
No option above works. The gap is logged with its annual value.
And the pooled order is what makes the investment possible. A one-off order does not finance a factory.
