Why it matters
PIF has more than 150 companies buying every day: steel, valves, cables, chemicals, equipment. A large share of it comes from abroad.
Case one
For a good reason
Genuinely nobody makes it locally.
Case two
For a sad reason
Someone makes it two hours away, and nobody knows they exist.
Case three
Because they do not fit
We found them, local and real. But their lead time is longer than the company can carry, or their quality sits under its bar. And every company sets a different bar.
And to this day, nobody can tell the three apart.
